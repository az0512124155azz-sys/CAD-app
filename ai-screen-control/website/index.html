Documentation

Learn how to use AI Screen Control

📸 Taking Screenshots

Click the camera button or press the keyboard shortcut to capture your screen instantly.

💬 Asking Questions

Type any question about what's on your screen. The AI will analyze the screenshot and answer.

🎮 Screen Control

Ask AI to click buttons, type text, or navigate. Say things like "Click the blue button" or "Type hello".

🎥 Video Analysis

Open a YouTube video and ask AI to analyze it. Requires Gemini API for best results.

⚙️ Settings

Configure API keys, enable/disable features, and customize your experience.

🔒 Privacy

All processing happens locally. Screenshots are only sent to your chosen AI API.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which API should I use?

Claude is recommended for general tasks. Gemini is best for video analysis. GPT works great for everything.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Screenshots are only sent to your chosen API provider. No data is stored on our servers.

Can I use multiple APIs?

Yes! You can configure multiple APIs and switch between them.

Does it work offline?

No, you need an internet connection to use AI features.
